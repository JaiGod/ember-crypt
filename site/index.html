EMBER CRYPT

Descend a crypt that rebuilds itself every run. Your torch is your life clock: it burns down as you explore, and in the dark the crypt bites back. Gather embers, light braziers, swap weapons, and face a boss every fifth floor. Death is permanent.

Play in browser
Free · no install · keyboard, mouse or touch · around 20 MB first load

Features

Procedural floors

Rooms and corridors are generated from scratch, with pillars, loops and a fresh layout every floor.

Torch-light radius

Light shrinks as your fuel burns. Embers refill it, braziers rekindle it, and with no fuel you take damage.

Melee and dash

Swing, slash or thrust with hit-stop and knockback, and dash through attacks with brief invulnerability.

Four weapons

Ember Sword, Cleaver Axe, Bone Spear and Cinder Wand, each with its own arc, reach and rhythm.

Blessings

Pick one of three upgrades between floors: max HP, damage, fuel, speed, sparks, lifesteal and more.

Bosses and permadeath

The Cinder Warden waits on floor 5 and the Ashen King on floor 10. Every run ends with a summary of how it went.

Denizens of the crypt

Skitter: fast, erratic swarmer Ember Ooze: hops, splits in two Bone Knight: telegraphed lunge Wisp: keeps distance, fires orbs Shade: blinks behind you, steals fuel

Made with

Godot 4.3 and GDScript. Every sprite, tile and effect is drawn in code with _draw(), and every sound is synthesised into PCM at startup. The game uses no image or audio files. The Web build is exported by GitHub Actions (godot-ci) and deployed to GitHub Pages.
